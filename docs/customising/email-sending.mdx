# Send with your own mail server

Every email the app sends leaves with a sender identity you control, and on Pro and above it can
leave from your own mail server instead of the app's. By the end of this page your emails arrive
under your name, and from your own address if you run a server for it.

## Before you start

- Access to the app **Settings** page.
- For your own server: its host, port and credentials, from whoever provides your mailbox.

## Where sending is configured

1. Open the app.
2. Select **Settings** in the app navigation.
3. Open the **Email sending** card, the first one on the page.

Everything in this card applies to your whole store: **Applies to every quiz in your store: result
emails and app notifications all go out with this identity.** There is no per quiz sender.

A badge at the top of the card names the route your emails take right now. It reads **Sending via:
app default** while the app sends them, and **Sending via: your SMTP server** once your own server is
configured, tested and switched on. Read that badge rather than your settings: it reflects what the
app would actually do with the next email, plan included.

## Set the sender identity

Two fields, in the **Sender** section, available on every plan.

1. Enter the name customers should see in **Sender name**.
2. Enter your own address in **Sender email address**.
3. Select **Save**.

On the default route, those two are used differently, which the field says out loud: **Without your
own SMTP server, emails are sent from the app's infrastructure: your sender name is applied, but your
email address is only used as the Reply-To.**

So a customer sees your store's name in their inbox, and a reply reaches the address you set here.
The sending address itself belongs to the app, because a mail service will not send from a domain it
has not verified, and your domain is verified with your own provider rather than with the app's.

Leave **Sender email address** empty and there is no reply address at all: a customer who replies
writes to the app's own sending address, which is not a mailbox you read. Fill it in even if you
never intend to use your own server.

## Use your own mail server <PlanBadge plan="pro" />

On Pro and above, **Use your own SMTP server** opens the section that replaces the default route.
Your server sends the message, and your address is what a customer sees in the From field rather than
in a reply header.

Below Pro the section shows a banner in its place, with a link to the plans.

### Enter your server details

1. Open **Use your own SMTP server**.
2. Enter **SMTP host** and **Port**.
3. Choose **Encryption**: **STARTTLS (usually port 587)** or **SSL/TLS (usually port 465)**.
4. Choose **Authentication**: **Login**, **Plain**, **CRAM-MD5** or **No authentication**.
5. Enter **Username** and **Password**, unless you chose **No authentication**, which disables both.
6. Select **Save**.

Your password is stored encrypted and never shown again. Once one is stored, the field reads **A
password is stored (encrypted). Leave blank to keep it.**, so you can edit a port or a username
without retyping it.

### Run the connection test

The card states the order to work in: **Enter your server details and save, then run a connection
test. Sending can be enabled once the test succeeds.**

1. Select **Test connection**.
2. Read the result, and check the inbox of your sender address.

The test is a real send. It connects to your server with the details you saved and sends a message to
your **Sender email address**, so a success means the route works end to end rather than that the
port is open. On success a banner confirms it and a green badge appears next to the button, reading
**Connection verified** with the date. On failure a red banner gives the reason your server returned,
which is usually a wrong port, a refused password, or an encryption mode the server does not offer.

**Test connection** is disabled while you have unsaved changes, with a note reading **Save your
changes before testing.**, since the test uses what is stored rather than what is on screen.

### Switch sending over

Tick **Enable sending via my SMTP server**, then select **Save**.

The checkbox stays disabled until a test has passed, explaining itself as **Run a successful
connection test first. Sending can only be enabled once your server is verified.** The app enforces
the same rule when saving, so the setting cannot be turned on by any other route.

Four things must all be true before an email actually leaves through your server: your plan includes
the feature, a connection test has passed, the checkbox is on, and both a host and a sender address
are set. Anything short of that sends through the app instead, quietly and without losing an email.
The badge at the top of the card is where you confirm which of the two is happening.

Editing any connection detail afterwards, the host, the port, the username, the password, the
encryption or the authentication mode, clears the verification and switches sending back to the app.
The checkbox then explains itself as **You changed connection settings: save and run the test again
before enabling.** Save, test again, then tick it again.

## What happens if you change plan

A configuration is never deleted when a plan changes. If you move below Pro, your host, credentials
and sender address stay stored, the server section is replaced by the upgrade banner, and sending
returns to the app's default route: your sender name is still applied and your address is still the
reply address, so customers see no interruption. The badge goes back to **Sending via: app default**.

Move up again and your configuration comes back as you left it, verification included. Nothing is
retyped.

## Which emails this covers

Everything the app sends for your store: the result emails your customers receive, the test emails
you send yourself from the **Result email** card, and the notifications the app writes to you. They
all leave by the route this card decides, so a working server is worth testing once and forgetting.

What those emails contain, and how the result email is turned on for a quiz, is set elsewhere, see
[Result email](/docs/customising/result-email).
