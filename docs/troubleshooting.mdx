# Troubleshooting

The symptoms merchants report most often, each with the cause behind it and the steps that resolve
it. Start here when the quiz does not behave the way you configured it.

## The quiz does not appear on my online store

The page where you placed the quiz shows nothing, or a message where the quiz should be.

Read the page as a customer first, because the two causes look different. An empty space, with
nothing at all where the quiz should be, means the block found no quiz to show: that is what an
unconfigured block renders, and it is not the unavailable message covered in the next section. A
visible message means the block named a quiz and was refused it.

1. Check the status of the quiz. A quiz in **Draft** is not served, and the block shows **Quiz
   temporarily unavailable. Please try again.** in its place. Open the quiz, select **Publish**, and
   check that its badge reads **Active**, see
   [Add the theme block](/docs/getting-started/theme-block).
2. Check that the block is still on the page. It reaches your online store only once the theme is
   saved, on every template you placed it on. Reopen the theme editor, find the **myDiagnostic Quiz**
   block in the left sidebar, and select **Save**.
3. Check the **Quiz ID** in the block settings. An empty field makes the block ask the app which quiz
   is published, which only works when exactly one is. Paste the Quiz ID of the quiz you want, copied
   from the **Install on your storefront** card of its **Settings** tab.
4. Count your published quizzes if you left the field empty. With several published, the block shows
   nothing at all rather than guessing, and the theme editor preview says so, reading **You have 2
   quizzes published. Paste the Quiz ID of the one to show in the block settings.** with your own
   number. Paste a Quiz ID, or unpublish until one remains.
5. Wait a minute after publishing. With an empty **Quiz ID** field the app's answer is kept
   for one minute, so a freshly published quiz can take that long to appear. Reload the page before
   changing anything else.

## Visitors see a message saying the quiz is unavailable

Where the quiz should be, customers read **Quiz temporarily unavailable. Please try again.**

1. Check your monthly response limit. Once it is reached, quizzes stop accepting answers for the rest
   of the month and every visitor gets that message. Open **Plans & Billing** and read **Responses
   this month**: at the limit, upgrade, or wait for the counter to reset with the calendar month, see
   [Plans and quotas](/docs/account/plans-and-quotas).
2. Check that the quiz is published if the message appears for one quiz only. The same message covers
   a block pointing at a quiz still in **Draft**. Publish it, see
   [Add the theme block](/docs/getting-started/theme-block).
3. Check your scoring if it happens at the end of a run rather than at the start. When no product
   collects a point, or every product that did is excluded, there is no result to show and the
   customer reads the same sentence. Attach products to more answers, or narrow your exclusions, see
   [Product matching](/docs/building-quizzes/product-matching).

## The quiz shows the wrong products

The recommendation is not the one you expected from those answers.

1. Check which answers carry the products. Only a product attached to an answer collects points, so a
   product attached to nothing can never win. Open each answer and look at its product list, see
   [Product matching](/docs/building-quizzes/product-matching).
2. Check the weights. A product on **+1** gains a third of what a product on **+3** gains from the
   same answer, and points add up across every answer the customer picked. Raise or lower the weight
   on the answers that should decide the result.
3. Check your exclusions. An answer with **This answer excludes products** turned on removes its
   products before the points are counted, whatever they collected, which promotes the next product
   in line. Turn the toggle off, or attach different products to that answer.
4. Check the answers a customer actually picked, on the **Responses** tab, before changing the
   weights. A result that surprises you often comes from a path you did not anticipate rather than
   from a wrong weight.

## The result always shows the same generic page

Customers get their products, with no description, no tips and no image.

1. Check that a scenario exists for the trio the answers produce. A scenario is shown only when the
   three winning products are exactly the three of its trio, in any order. Any other combination
   lands on the generic result, which is normal behaviour rather than an error, see
   [Result scenarios](/docs/building-quizzes/scenarios).
2. Check the size of the trio against the size of the result. A scenario of three products is never
   shown against a result of two, and the app warns you on a shorter scenario with **Incomplete trios
   rarely match: visitors will see the generic fallback.** Complete the trio, see
   [Product matching](/docs/building-quizzes/product-matching).
3. Check your plan. Scenario editorial needs Basic or above: on Free your scenarios are kept and
   listed but never served, so every customer gets the generic result, see
   [Plans and quotas](/docs/account/plans-and-quotas).

## The colours or fonts do not follow my settings

A screen ignores what you set in the **Design** tab.

1. Check whether that page sets its own value. A per page setting always wins over the design tokens.
   Open the page in the editor and use the **Reset** next to the setting to hand it back, see
   [Design and tokens](/docs/customising/design-tokens).
2. Check the granular controls. A value inside **Granular controls (advanced)** counts as soon as you
   moved it away from its default, and it beats the token above it. Use **Reset granular controls to
   defaults** to clear the whole block at once.
3. Check your custom CSS if you wrote any. A rule there is applied last and can override a token
   without warning, and a rule the browser cannot make sense of is ignored in silence. Empty the
   field, save, and reload the storefront to see whether the setting comes back.
4. Check the storefront rather than the preview for anything to do with custom CSS: the **Live
   preview** does not apply it, so a rule can look absent in the admin and work in the shop.

## The quiz looks right on desktop and wrong on mobile

The phone rendering ignores sizes that are correct on a large screen.

1. Check the **Mobile responsive** card first. Its six values apply below 768 px whether or not you
   touched them, so the question size, the answer size, the button padding, the content padding and
   the question image settings come from there on a phone, not from your typography settings, see
   [Design and tokens](/docs/customising/design-tokens).
2. Check the mobile twin of the setting on the page itself. A mobile control left alone inherits the
   desktop value, and one you moved keeps its own value until you use its **Reset**, see
   [Welcome screen](/docs/building-quizzes/welcome-screen).
3. Check the height. Screens are not made uniform on phones: their height follows their content
   unless you set **Minimum height (mobile)** under **Quiz height**.

## No email arrives

A customer finished the quiz and received nothing.

1. Check that sending is on for that quiz. **Send results by email** is per quiz and off by default,
   in the quiz **Settings** tab, see [Result email](/docs/customising/result-email).
2. Check that the quiz has its email gate enabled. The email is only sent to the address entered at
   the gate: an address collected by a welcome capture field is stored for your exports and never
   triggers it, see [Email gate](/docs/building-quizzes/email-gate).
3. Check the spam folder of the test address, then send a test from the **Result email** card. A
   failed test answers with the reason in a red banner, which is the one place a delivery problem is
   reported back to you.
4. Check your mail server if you configured one. Sending stops going through it as soon as a
   credential changes, and the badge at the top of the **Email sending** card tells you which route
   your emails take, see [Send with your own mail server](/docs/customising/email-sending).
5. Check nothing else about your plan: sending the result email is not gated. Every plan sends it,
   and what Pro adds is control over how it looks, see
   [Plan comparison](/docs/reference/plan-comparison).

## The email arrives with a broken layout

The message reaches the inbox but does not look like the preview.

1. Check the content width. The **Container** section of **Layout and formatting** accepts up to
   800 px, and the control names the safe value: **600px is the safe width for most mail clients.**
   Bring it back to 600 px, see [Result email](/docs/customising/result-email).
2. Check your logo width against the card. **Maximum logo width** goes up to 560 px while the email
   body is 600 px wide, so a logo near the top of that range leaves almost no margin. Lower it and
   refresh the preview.
3. Check whether images are blocked rather than broken. Many mail clients hide remote images until
   the reader allows them, which turns a logo and product photos into empty boxes on an otherwise
   correct layout. Ask your tester to display images and read the message again.
4. Check the font. The email uses only fonts already installed on the reader's device, so a message
   that renders in a different typeface than your quiz is behaving as designed rather than breaking.
5. Do not expect your custom CSS to help. It applies to the quiz on your online store and never to
   the email, which is built with the restricted set of formatting mail clients support.

## A product is sold out or missing from the result

A card shows as unavailable, or fewer products appear than you expected.

1. Check the product in Shopify. A product with no stock keeps its card, its title, its description
   and its price, gains a **Sold out** badge, and its button is disabled, see
   [Result screen](/docs/building-quizzes/result-screen).
2. Check the bundle button when one product of three is sold out. It stays active, adds the ones that
   can be bought, and a line under it explains that sold-out items are not added.
3. Check how many products collected points if a card is missing entirely. A result can hold one or
   two cards: only products attached to a picked answer collect points, and an excluded product is
   removed whatever it collected, see
   [Product matching](/docs/building-quizzes/product-matching).
4. Check that the product still exists in your catalogue. Prices and availability are read from
   Shopify each time a result is rendered, so a product you deleted or unpublished stops behaving
   like the one you attached.

## My second language is not served to visitors

Customers browsing in another language read the quiz in its default language.

1. Check your plan. Additional languages need Premium or above, and a store below it serves the
   default language only, see [Secondary languages](/docs/distribution/secondary-languages).
2. Check the **Languages** card of the quiz for a warning. A downgrade keeps the languages you added
   and stops serving those beyond your allowance, listing them under **Kept but not served on your
   current plan.** Upgrade, or remove the ones you no longer need.
3. Check that your online store actually serves that language. The quiz reads the language of the
   page it sits in, so a language your store does not offer never reaches the quiz.
4. Check the fields themselves. Adding a language adds translation tabs, it translates nothing: an
   untranslated field falls back to your original text, which reads as a language that is only
   half served.

## I cannot publish another quiz, or create one

The **Publish** action is refused, or the new quiz form opens on a warning.

1. Count your published quizzes. The limit counts published quizzes only, from 1 on Free to 25 on
   Premium, see [Plans and quotas](/docs/account/plans-and-quotas).
2. Unpublish a quiz to free a slot. Creation is refused on the same count, so unpublishing one makes
   both actions available again straight away.
3. Duplicate instead of creating, when you want a copy of an existing quiz. A duplicate arrives as a
   draft, so it takes no slot, and its Klaviyo and Mailchimp connections arrive switched off, see
   [Klaviyo and Mailchimp](/docs/distribution/integrations).
4. Keep as many drafts as you like. They are unlimited on every plan and count towards nothing.

## I cannot save, or my changes disappeared

A bar sits at the top of the screen, or work you did is not on the storefront.

1. Look for the bar at the top of the editor. It appears as soon as something changes and holds your
   work until you select **Save**, which writes the whole set at once, see
   [Questions and answers](/docs/building-quizzes/questions-and-answers).
2. Save or discard before adding, deleting or moving a page. Those controls are disabled while a
   change is pending, with the title **Save or discard your changes first**.
3. Know what **Discard** does before using it. It drops everything the bar was holding, and it does
   not undo the four things written immediately: adding a page, deleting a page, reordering pages and
   uploading an image. A question you added and then discarded stays in the quiz, empty, so delete it
   from the sidebar.
4. Do not leave the editor on a confirmation dialog you did not read. Leaving with a change pending
   loses everything the bar was holding.
