# Analytics

The **Analytics** tab counts the customers who finished a quiz and shows which result they were
given, over a period you choose. By the end of this page you can read every figure it holds and know
exactly what each one counts.

## Before you start

- A quiz open in the editor.
- At least one finished response, since every figure on the tab comes from customers who reached
  the result screen.

## Open the tab

1. Open the quiz from the app home.
2. Select **Analytics** in the row of tabs at the top, between **Design** and **Responses**.

The tab holds three things: the period filter, two figures side by side, and the **Scenario
distribution** card under them. Nothing on it is editable, and reading it changes nothing in the
quiz.

## Choose the period

A line reading **Period:** sits above the figures, followed by three choices. **Last 30 days** is
selected when you arrive.

- **Last 7 days**
- **Last 30 days**
- **All time**

The first two count backwards from the moment you open the page, so they are rolling windows rather
than calendar months. **Last 30 days** on 20 March covers 18 February to 20 March, and it will not
match the monthly usage figure on **Plans & Billing**, which resets on the first of each month.

Your choice drives the whole tab at once: both figures and every row of the distribution are counted
over the same window. It is not remembered. Leaving the tab and coming back starts on **Last 30
days** again.

## The two figures

**Total responses** is the number of customers who finished the quiz during the period.

**Emails collected** is how many of those responses carry an email address, and the line under it
gives the share, for example **62% of responses**. An address counts here whether the customer typed
it into the email gate or into an **Email** capture field on the welcome screen, see
[Email gate](/docs/building-quizzes/email-gate). The share is rounded to the nearest whole number.

## What counts as a response

A response is written at the end of the run, at the moment the result is about to appear on screen.
Everything the customer did before that point leaves no trace, which decides most of what these
figures mean.

- A customer who abandons at a question is not counted anywhere.
- With the email gate switched on, the gate stands between the last question and the result, so a
  customer who closes the page rather than giving an address is not counted either. **Total
  responses** is then the number of people who went through the gate.
- With the gate switched off, the result follows the last question directly, and the response is
  recorded there.
- Reopening a result later through its permalink shows the stored result and adds nothing, see
  [Permalink](/docs/distribution/permalink).
- When the answers return no product at all, because the products an answer voted for were deleted
  from the store, the customer gets the unavailability message instead of a result and nothing is
  recorded.
- Once your monthly response allowance is used up, the customers who finish after that get the same
  unavailability message, and they are not recorded. Fifty responses per month on the Free plan,
  counted for the whole store rather than per quiz, see
  [Plans and quotas](/docs/account/plans-and-quotas).

## The scenario distribution

The **Scenario distribution** card breaks the same responses down by the result each customer
landed on. One row per result, ordered from the most frequent down, each carrying its title, the
number of responses followed by **resp.**, the share of the period total as a percentage, and a bar drawn
to that percentage.

A scenario with no response in the period has no row at all. Widen the period to bring it back.

### The result is recorded, not recalculated

The scenario a customer saw is stored with their response, at the moment they saw it. It is never
worked out again afterwards. Change the products of a scenario, change the weights behind an answer,
add a question, and past responses stay in the row they were filed under. Only new responses follow
the new setup, which is what lets you compare a period before a change with a period after it.

Two edits do reach the rows already there:

- Renaming a scenario renames its row, including for the responses recorded under the old name. The
  card reads the title live, so it always shows the scenario as it is named today.
- Deleting a scenario does not delete its responses. They keep counting in **Total responses**, and
  they move into the **Generic result** row, since the result they were given no longer exists.

### The Generic result row

**Generic result** is the row for every response whose three winning products matched no scenario.
Those customers still got a result, with the same product cards and none of the editorial writing a
scenario adds, see [Result scenarios](/docs/building-quizzes/scenarios).

A large **Generic result** share is worth acting on: it says most of your customers reach a trio you
have written nothing for. On the Free plan, where scenarios are not available, it is the only row
the card can ever show.

## When there is nothing to show

When the period holds no response, the **Scenario distribution** card carries a message in place of
the bars. The two figures above it stay on screen and read 0.

On a published quiz the message is titled **No responses in this period** and reads **Analytics fill
in as customers complete this quiz on your storefront. Try a longer period, or take the quiz
yourself to generate a first response.**, with an **Open your store** button next to it. Taking the
quiz yourself does record a real response, which then counts in these figures and against your
monthly allowance like any other.

On a draft quiz the message is titled **No responses yet** and reads **This quiz is still a draft:
publish it and place the theme block to start collecting responses.**, with an **Open quiz Settings**
button. A draft quiz serves nothing on the storefront, so it can hold no response until you publish
it and place the block, see [Add the theme block](/docs/getting-started/theme-block).

## Exporting is done on the Responses tab

There is no export on **Analytics**. The responses themselves, one by one, and the **Export CSV**
button that downloads them live on the **Responses** tab of the same quiz, immediately to the right
of **Analytics** in the row of tabs.

The file holds one row per response, with the date, the name, the email address, the age, the
scenario, the recommended products and the link to the customer's result page. A response that
matched no scenario carries **Generic result** in its scenario column, exactly as on this tab. The
export always covers every response the quiz ever collected: the period you selected on
**Analytics** has no effect on it.

## Figures from long ago

Responses are anonymised automatically once they are 24 months old. The name, the email address, the
age, the language and the detailed answers are cleared, and the response itself is kept, with its
quiz, its scenario and its recommended products. **Total responses** and the distribution therefore
keep their full history, while **Emails collected** drops the addresses that aged out. See
[Data and privacy](/docs/account/data-and-privacy).

## The plan you need

The **Analytics** tab, its two figures and the scenario distribution are available on every plan,
Free included.

One thing on it depends on your plan. Scenarios need the Basic plan or above, so a quiz on Free has
none to show, and its distribution is a single **Generic result** row holding every response.

See [Plans and quotas](/docs/account/plans-and-quotas) for what else each plan carries.
