# Create your first quiz

A new quiz starts empty: you give it a name and a language, and everything the customer sees is
yours to build. By the end of this page your quiz holds a question, meets the two conditions the app
checks before publishing, and its badge reads **Active**.

## Before you start

- myDiagnostic installed in your Shopify admin.
- At least one product in your online store, so an answer has something to point at.

## Create the quiz

1. Open myDiagnostic in your Shopify admin.
2. Select **Create quiz** at the top of the quiz list.
3. Enter a name in **Quiz name**.
4. Choose a language in **Default language**.
5. Select **Create quiz** to confirm.

The form asks for those two things and nothing else. Everything else about the quiz is set later, in
the quiz itself.

**Quiz name** carries the help text **Internal name, only visible in the admin.** The name
identifies the quiz for you, in your quiz list and across the app, and it is used in the subject of
the result email your customer receives. It never appears in the address of any page of your online
store, so no customer reaches the quiz through it. You can change it later.

**Default language** carries the help text **The primary language for quiz content.** It is the
language you write the quiz in, and the one a customer is served when no translation matches their
own.

## What a new quiz contains

You land on the **Questions** tab of the quiz you have created, and its badge reads **Draft**.
A quiz in **Draft** is never served to your online store.

The left sidebar already holds two entries, **Email gate** and **Results**. They sit at the bottom of
the list, they cannot be moved or deleted, and selecting either one opens its settings. Above them
the list reads **No question or welcome page yet.** There is no welcome screen, no question and no
scenario: the app creates none of them for you.

The middle of the screen shows no preview, because there is nothing yet to preview. It reads **Add
your first question**, followed by **A quiz recommends products by counting the votes its answers
carry, so it needs at least one question, with products on its answers, before it can be
published.**, and it carries an **Add a question** button.

The panel on the right reads **Select a question, answer, or scenario to edit its settings** until
you select something in the sidebar.

## The six tabs of a quiz

- **Questions** is where you build the quiz: the sequence of pages on the left, a live preview in the
  middle, and the settings of whatever you select on the right.
- **Scenarios** is where you write the editorial scenarios, each one pairing a trio of products with
  your own title, description and tips. Editorial scenarios need the Basic plan or above: on the
  Free plan the left sidebar shows **Unlock scenarios** in place of **Add scenario**, and what a
  scenario holds is covered in [Result scenarios](/docs/building-quizzes/scenarios).
- **Design** holds the colours, the fonts and the layout the whole quiz is drawn with, with a live
  preview beside them.
- **Analytics** counts **Total responses** and **Emails collected** over a period you choose, and
  shows the **Scenario distribution** behind them.
- **Responses** lists the responses one by one and exports them with **Export CSV**.
- **Settings** holds the quiz name, the **Behavior** card with **Auto-advance on answer**, the
  languages the quiz answers in, the result email, the connections to Klaviyo and Mailchimp, and the
  **Install on your storefront** card.

## Add your first question

Two routes lead to the same result, and both are available at any time.

### From the empty screen

1. Select **Add a question** in the middle of the **Questions** tab.

### From the sidebar

1. Select **Add page** at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Select **Question**, described in the menu as **Ask the visitor something**.

The same menu offers **Welcome**, described as **Intro screen with optional capture fields**. That
entry disappears from the menu once the quiz has a welcome screen, since a quiz holds one at most.

A new question arrives named **New question**, with no answers yet. Writing the question, adding its
answers and choosing how they are displayed to the customer is covered in
[Questions and answers](/docs/building-quizzes/questions-and-answers).

## Publish the quiz

A quiz goes live only when it can return a recommendation. The app checks two conditions, both of
them required, on the quiz as it is saved.

- **At least one question.** Until there is one, the reason reads **Add at least one question before
  publishing: a quiz with no question cannot recommend anything.**
- **At least one product attached to an answer that does not exclude products.** Until there is one,
  the reason reads **Assign at least one product to an answer before publishing: without a product
  vote the result screen has nothing to show.**

An answer that excludes products carries them only to take them out of the running, so a quiz whose
product-bearing answers all exclude has nothing left to recommend, and the second condition is not
met. Attaching products to answers, and excluding them, is covered in
[Product matching](/docs/building-quizzes/product-matching).

While a condition is unmet, **Publish** stays disabled and names the condition that is missing.
Hover the button to read it, or open the **Install on your storefront** card in the **Settings** tab,
where the same sentence replaces the usual status line.

Once both conditions are met:

1. Save any change still pending in the editor.
2. Select **Publish** at the top of the quiz.
3. Read the confirmation, titled **Publish this quiz?**, which reads **The quiz will be live on your
   storefront for any theme block referencing this Quiz ID.**
4. Select **Publish** to confirm.

The badge moves from **Draft** to **Active**. Select **Unpublish** to take a live quiz back to
**Draft** at any time.

A published quiz still needs the app block placed on your online store before a customer can see it,
which is covered in [Add the theme block](/docs/getting-started/theme-block).

## The limit on published quizzes

Your plan limits how many quizzes are **Active** at the same time. Drafts are not counted, so you can
keep as many of them as you want, and a quiz costs you nothing until you publish it. The Free plan
allows one published quiz.

At the limit, **Create quiz** is disabled in the quiz list, and a panel there offers **View plans**.
The new quiz form, if you reach it anyway, shows a warning that reads **Drafts are unlimited.
Unpublish one of your live quizzes to free a slot, or upgrade to publish more at once.**, with an
**Upgrade plan** button beside it.

To publish a different quiz without changing plan:

1. Open the quiz that is currently **Active**.
2. Select **Unpublish**.
3. Open the quiz you want to publish and select **Publish**.

## Duplicate a quiz

Duplicating gives you a working copy to change without touching the quiz your customers are
answering.

1. Open the quiz list.
2. Select the menu at the end of the row of the quiz.
3. Select **Duplicate**.

The **Duplicate** button at the top of the quiz editor does the same thing and opens the copy. The
copy is made from the saved quiz, so save first: an editor with unsaved changes refuses and shows
**Save your changes before duplicating**.

The copy is named after the original with **(copy)** added to the end, and it arrives in **Draft**,
so it takes no published slot until you publish it. It carries the questions, the answers, the
products attached to those answers, the scenarios and the translations.

Two things do not follow the copy.

- The responses already collected stay with the original quiz. The copy starts at zero, and so do its
  analytics.
- The Klaviyo and Mailchimp connections arrive switched off. The list and the tag are kept, and the
  connection has to be tested again before it can be switched back on, see
  [Klaviyo and Mailchimp](/docs/distribution/integrations).

## Delete a quiz

Deleting is done from the quiz list, on one quiz or on a selection of several. There is no delete
button inside a quiz.

1. Open the quiz list.
2. Select the menu at the end of the row of the quiz.
3. Select **Delete**.
4. Type the name of the quiz in the confirmation field.
5. Select **Delete** to confirm.

To delete several at once, tick them in the list, select **Delete selected**, and type **delete** in
the confirmation field.

Deleting takes the quiz and everything attached to it: its questions and its answers, the products
attached to those answers, its scenarios, its translations, and every response it has collected,
including the email addresses in them. The result links already sent to your customers by email stop
working, because the response each one opens no longer exists. None of it can be recovered, and the
confirmation says as much: **This cannot be undone.**
