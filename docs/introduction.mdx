# Introduction

myDiagnostic is a Shopify app that turns a guided quiz into a personalised product recommendation,
with no code required. A customer answers a few questions and reaches a result screen carrying the
three products their answers chose, taken from the products already in your online store.

A quiz is a sequence of screens you build yourself: an optional welcome screen, your questions, an
optional email gate, and the result screen. Every answer votes for the products you attach to it,
with a weight of **+1**, **+2** or **+3**, and an answer can instead exclude the products it never
fits, so the three products holding the most votes are the ones the result shows. From the Basic
plan a scenario answers a precise trio with an editorial page of its own, its title, description,
image and tips, and a trio you wrote no scenario for still reaches the generic result. Every
finished quiz is recorded as a response with a permanent address that reopens the same
recommendation later, and the app can email that recommendation to the customer as soon as they
finish.

To begin you need a Shopify store with at least one product and an Online Store 2.0 theme, the only
theme type that accepts app blocks. Install the app from the Shopify App Store, see
[Install the app](/docs/getting-started/installation), then place the **myDiagnostic Quiz** block in
a section of the page you want it on, see [Add the theme block](/docs/getting-started/theme-block).
The app opens the theme editor with that block already added and already selected, and it asks for
no access to your theme files.

From there, Building quizzes covers what a quiz contains, starting with
[Questions and answers](/docs/building-quizzes/questions-and-answers). Customising covers how it
looks and how it reads, starting with [Design and tokens](/docs/customising/design-tokens).
Reference holds the complete plan grid, in [Plan comparison](/docs/reference/plan-comparison).
