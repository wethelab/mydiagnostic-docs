# Plan comparison

The five plans side by side: what each one costs, how much it lets you run, and which features it
unlocks. This is the complete grid, so nothing on this page is repeated anywhere else.

| | Free | Basic | Pro | Premium | Unlimited |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price per month, in US dollars | $0 | $29 | $79 | $159 | $249 |
| Free trial | None | 14 days | 14 days | 14 days | 14 days |
| Published quizzes at once | 1 | 3 | 10 | 25 | Unlimited |
| Responses per month | 50 | 750 | 3,000 | 10,000 | Unlimited |
| Additional languages per quiz | None | None | None | 1 | Unlimited |
| myDiagnostic badge under the quiz | Shown | Removed | Removed | Removed | Removed |
| Editorial scenarios | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Custom CSS | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Klaviyo and Mailchimp | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Send through your own mail server | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Result email logo, colours and wording | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Drafts are not counted in the quiz row: you may keep as many as you like on any plan, and only a
published quiz takes one of the slots above.

The trial is Shopify's own, applied the first time you subscribe to a paid plan, and it runs once:
a later change of plan does not open a second one. Free carries none because it is permanent and
asks for no card.

Three fields of the result email are outside every gate, on Free as much as on Unlimited: the privacy
policy link, the terms link and the notification address. Pointing your customers at your own legal
pages, and receiving what the app has to send you, are not things a plan should decide, see
[Result email](/docs/customising/result-email).

Sending the result email is not gated either. Every plan sends it; what Pro adds is control over how
it looks and what it says.

For what happens when you reach a limit, how the counter resets, and what a change of plan does to
your published quizzes, see [Plans and quotas](/docs/account/plans-and-quotas).
