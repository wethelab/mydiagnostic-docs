# Glossary

Every term this documentation uses for a part of the app, a part of the Shopify admin, or a step of
the quiz, defined once so you never have to guess what a page means.

## Answer

One of the choices a customer picks on a question. An answer carries its own wording, the products it
votes for and their weight, and it can instead be set to exclude products, see
[Questions and answers](/docs/building-quizzes/questions-and-answers).

## App block

The piece of myDiagnostic you place inside a page of your online store, called **myDiagnostic Quiz**.
It is not a standalone page: you add it to a section of an existing template, and the quiz renders
where you put it, see [Add the theme block](/docs/getting-started/theme-block).

## App embed

A Shopify extension a theme loads on every page of a store, switched on from the **App embeds** list
of the theme editor. myDiagnostic has none: the quiz is placed page by page as an app block, so an
empty App embeds list is expected and not a sign of a failed install, see
[Add the theme block](/docs/getting-started/theme-block).

## App wording

The text the app supplies by itself, such as **Next**, **Sold out** or the message under an invalid
email address, which you can override for your whole store and per language. It is the opposite of
page content: page content is what you write on a quiz, and it always wins over app wording for the
same string, see [App wording](/docs/customising/app-wording).

## Capture field

A box on the welcome screen that asks the customer for something before the quiz starts: a name, an
age, an email address, a phone number, or a field of your own. Each field decides where its value is
stored and whether it is required, see [Welcome screen](/docs/building-quizzes/welcome-screen).

## Default language

The language a quiz is written in, chosen when the quiz is created. It cannot be changed afterwards,
it is what every untranslated string falls back to, and it is the language every customer reads when
their own is not served, see [Secondary languages](/docs/distribution/secondary-languages).

## Design token

One of the store-wide appearance settings of a quiz: a colour, a font, a corner style, an animation
setting. Tokens apply to the whole quiz, and a per page setting overrides them for one screen, see
[Design and tokens](/docs/customising/design-tokens).

## Email gate

The screen shown between the last question and the result, asking the customer for their email
address before the recommendation. It is what makes the result email possible, and the only screen
carrying the marketing consent checkbox, see [Email gate](/docs/building-quizzes/email-gate).

## Exclusion

An answer set to rule products out instead of voting for them. An exclusion is absolute: a product
ruled out by any answer the customer picked is removed before the points are counted, however many
votes it collected, see [Product matching](/docs/building-quizzes/product-matching).

## Generic result

The result a customer gets when the three winning products match no scenario: the quiz's own result
title and subtitle over the three product cards, with no editorial text and no tips. The response is
recorded like any other, see [Result scenarios](/docs/building-quizzes/scenarios).

## Permalink

The permanent address of one customer's result, of the form
`https://your-store.com/apps/mydiagnostic/result/<token>`. It reopens the same recommendation, it
does not expire, and it is what the button in the result email points at, see
[Result permalink](/docs/distribution/permalink).

## Plan

Your subscription level, from Free to Unlimited. It decides how many quizzes you may keep published,
how many responses your quizzes may collect in a month, and which features you can use, see
[Plans and quotas](/docs/account/plans-and-quotas).

## Product trio

The three products a scenario is attached to. A scenario is shown only when the three products a
customer's answers produced are exactly the three of its trio, in any order, see
[Result scenarios](/docs/building-quizzes/scenarios).

## Question

One screen of a quiz, holding the wording the customer reads and the answers they choose from. A
question is either **Single choice** or **Multiple choice**, and it decides how its own answers are
laid out, see [Questions and answers](/docs/building-quizzes/questions-and-answers).

## Quiz

One questionnaire: its pages, its questions and answers, its scenarios, its design and its own
settings. A store can hold several, and your plan decides how many of them may be published at once,
see [Create your first quiz](/docs/getting-started/first-quiz).

## Quiz ID

The identifier you paste into the theme block so it knows which quiz to render, copied for you by the
**Install on your storefront** card. It is not the slug: the slug is the readable form the app
derives from the quiz name and never asks you to type, which is why renaming a quiz carries the note
**The public URL slug does not change.**, while the Quiz ID is the only identifier a screen ever asks
you for, see [Add the theme block](/docs/getting-started/theme-block).

## Response

One completed run of a quiz by one customer: their answers, whatever they filled in, and the result
they were given. A response is recorded when they submit, it counts against your monthly limit, and
it is what the **Responses** tab lists, see [Plans and quotas](/docs/account/plans-and-quotas).

## Result screen

The last screen of a quiz: the product cards, the add to cart buttons and the copy around them.
Every result renders in it, whether or not a scenario filled it with editorial content, see
[Result screen](/docs/building-quizzes/result-screen).

## Scenario

A result page you write yourself, attached to one precise trio of products, with a title, a
description, an image and tips. The result screen is the frame, the scenario is what goes in it for
that particular trio, see [Result scenarios](/docs/building-quizzes/scenarios).

## Scenario tip

One short piece of advice listed under a scenario's description, each with its own text and icon.
Tips belong to a scenario, so a customer only reads them when that scenario matched, see
[Result scenarios](/docs/building-quizzes/scenarios).

## Secondary language

A language a quiz serves in addition to its default language. Adding one adds a translation tab next
to every translatable field, and your plan decides how many a quiz may serve, see
[Secondary languages](/docs/distribution/secondary-languages).

## Theme editor

The Shopify screen where you build the pages of your online store, and where the quiz block is
placed and moved. The app opens it for you, with **Open the theme editor** in the **Install on your
storefront** card, see [Add the theme block](/docs/getting-started/theme-block).

## Weight

How much a product gains when a customer picks the answer it is attached to: **+1**, **+2** or
**+3**. A weight sizes a vote, where an exclusion cancels one: points decide the ranking, exclusions
remove a product from it entirely, see [Product matching](/docs/building-quizzes/product-matching).
